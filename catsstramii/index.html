Four boards, one field notebook

From strategies to scenes, scores, and trials — organized in a local library.

A

Atlas

Strategy Atlas holds every play: title, method, tags, pins, and review due dates — a searchable map of what works.

F

Field

Field map records garage, garden, alley, and lobby scenes, pins strategies to real places, and keeps reuse logs.

P

Pulse

Score strategies, watch waves and distributions, and see which plays still earn their keep.

G

Forge

Change a play on purpose: hypothesis, variable, status, and conclusion — so trials do not scatter into notes.
