Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CatsStramii handles information related to you. By using the app, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Who we are

CatsStramii is an on-device strategy atlas for logging plays, scenes, reuse records, scores, and trials. Core data stays on your device. The app does not offer cloud account sync and does not upload reference photos you attach.

2. Information we process

CatsStramii is designed for local use. It may process content you enter and store on the device, such as:

  • Strategy and field records: titles, methods, tags, scene environments, reuse outcomes, scores, trial hypotheses and conclusions, and other text you provide;
  • Reference photos: only when you choose Take Photo or Choose from Library to attach a visual reference to a record. Photos remain on this iPhone and are not uploaded;
  • App preferences: settings such as pin-first sorting, review interval, and compact card layout.

We do not require a cloud account, and we do not upload these notebook contents to a server to provide core features.

3. Camera and Photo Library access

The app requests camera or photo library access only when you actively attach a reference photo while editing a play, scene, reuse log, score, or trial. Declining permission does not block other local logging features. You can enable access later in Settings.

4. How we use information

Locally stored information is used only to:

  • display and maintain Atlas, Field, Pulse, and Forge on this device;
  • remember your preferences and improve on-device usability;
  • export a local library backup when you choose to do so.

We do not use your records for targeted ads or profiling, and we do not sell them to third parties.

5. Sharing and disclosure

CatsStramii does not operate a cloud account service for syncing your notebook, so we do not routinely collect or share your strategy content. We may disclose information only when required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect CatsStramii, our users, or the public, to the extent permitted by law.

6. Third-party services

Core features do not rely on advertising, payment, or analytics SDKs. App Store distribution is handled by Apple under its own policies and is outside this app’s control.

7. Payments and subscriptions

CatsStramii currently does not offer in-app purchases, subscriptions, or online payments, and does not collect or store bank card or other payment credentials through the app.

8. Data security

Data is stored locally on your device. Please protect your device and use system lock and permission controls as needed. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed if a device is lost or compromised by malware.

9. Retention and deletion

Strategies, scenes, scores, trials, and reference photos remain on your device until you delete the related records in the app, or until you uninstall the app and the system clears app data. Exported backup files are yours to keep.

10. Your choices

Because data primarily stays on-device, you can view, edit, or delete records directly in the app. If you have questions about this policy, contact us using the details below.

11. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child used the app on a device in a way that needs help, please contact us.

12. International transfers

Core notebook data and photos are stored on your device by default and are not actively transmitted by us to servers in other countries or regions.

13. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page or communicated in another appropriate way. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

14. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

support@catsstramii.app
