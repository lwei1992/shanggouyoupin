What to include

Please include the app name CatsStramii, app version, system version (for example iOS), and a clear description of the issue. Screenshots without sensitive information can help.

Do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive data.

Privacy and data requests

Strategies and photos in CatsStramii stay on your device by default. You can view, edit, or delete records directly in the app. Privacy questions can go to the same support email.

For the full policy, see the Privacy Policy.

Try before you write

  • Update the app and system to the latest versions;
  • Restart the app or device;
  • Confirm Camera / Photos permissions in Settings when you need to attach a reference photo;
  • Reproduce the issue and note the exact steps.

FAQ

  • How do I download the app? Search for CatsStramii on the App Store, or use the download link on this site.
  • Does data get uploaded? Core strategies and reference photos stay on-device. The app does not upload them.
  • Why camera or photo library access? Only when you choose to attach a reference photo to a record. Declining permission does not block other features.
  • How do I back up? Export the local library from the app and keep the file somewhere safe.
